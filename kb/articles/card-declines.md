# Card payment declined

## Common decline codes
- 05 Do not honour: the issuer blocked the transaction, often a fraud rule or a merchant-category block.
- 51 Insufficient funds: available balance (not ledger balance) is too low. Pending authorisations reduce the available balance for up to 7 days.
- 54 Expired card: the card has expired or the merchant stored an old expiry date.
- 57 Transaction not permitted: online, international or contactless use is switched off in Card Controls.
- 61 Exceeds limit: the daily purchase or ATM limit is reached.

## What the customer can check
1. Open Cards > Card Controls and confirm online, international and contactless payments are enabled.
2. Compare the available balance with the payment amount, including pending authorisations.
3. Confirm the card is active and not temporarily frozen.
4. Retry once after 10 minutes; repeated retries can trigger fraud rules.

## When to escalate
- Code 51 while the available balance clearly covers the payment: escalate to the Cards team, priority high.
- Code 05 at three or more merchants in one day: possible fraud-rule block, escalate to Fraud Operations, priority high.
- Customer reports payments they did not make: follow the disputes process and freeze the card, priority critical.
