# International transfers (SWIFT)

## Cut-off and timing
- Transfers submitted before 1:00 PM Bahrain time, Sunday to Thursday, are sent the same business day.
- Delivery normally takes 1 to 3 business days depending on the correspondent bank and destination country.
- Public holidays in Bahrain or the destination country add delays.

## Tracking a transfer
- Every SWIFT transfer has a UETR reference shown in the transfer details screen.
- Agents can request a gpi trace using the UETR; results usually arrive within 4 business hours.
- Missing or wrong beneficiary details (IBAN, name mismatch) are the most common reason for returns.

## Returned or delayed transfers
- Returned funds are credited back minus correspondent fees, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
- Delays over 5 business days with no gpi update: escalate to Payments Operations, priority high.
- Large transfers held for compliance review: do not share review details with the customer, priority medium.
