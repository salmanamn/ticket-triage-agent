# Mobile app crashes or will not open

## Supported devices
- The app supports iOS 17 and later and Android 10 and later.
- Older OS versions can install previous app versions but stop receiving security updates.

## Troubleshooting
1. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Restart the device and free at least 500 MB of storage.
3. Reinstall the app; the customer must log in again with password and OTP.

## When to escalate
- Crash continues after reinstall: collect device model, OS version and app version, then escalate to Mobile Engineering, priority medium.
- Crash blocks access to funds for many customers after a release: major incident, priority critical.
