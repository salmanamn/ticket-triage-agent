# Disputed and unrecognised card transactions

## Unrecognised transactions
- Freeze the card immediately in the app (Cards > Freeze) or ask an agent to freeze it.
- Treat any report of transactions the customer did not make as possible fraud, priority critical.
- Issue a replacement card; the old card must not be unfrozen.

## Merchant disputes
- For goods not received, duplicate charges or wrong amounts, the customer contacts the merchant first.
- If the merchant does not resolve it within 15 days, raise a chargeback with receipts and correspondence.
- Card scheme time limits apply; most disputes must be raised within 120 days of the transaction.

## Duplicate charges
- A pending duplicate authorisation usually drops off within 7 days without action.
- Two posted (settled) charges for one purchase: raise a dispute, priority medium.
